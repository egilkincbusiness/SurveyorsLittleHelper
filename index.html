    Before you use this

    Surveyor's Little Helper is an unofficial finding aid. It is not made by, endorsed by, or connected to the Nebraska State Surveyor's Office.

    • It helps you find survey records. It is not a survey and cannot establish a boundary, a corner, or a legal description.
    • Section outlines come from state mapping data and are approximate. Phone GPS can be off by many feet.
    • The list may be incomplete or out of date, and a survey can be placed in the wrong section. Always check the official repository and the original records.
    • Survey images belong to the state repository and open from the state's website.

    Do not use it alone for legal, boundary, or construction decisions.
